A GUIDE TO THE LENS
One model. Four passes.
Ouro reuses 48 transformer layers four times. Each grid row is a distinct invocation: a loop and a physical layer. Each column is the state after reading one input token.
Choose a readout
J-lens transports the state to its own loop’s final-layer coordinates before unembedding. Logit lens unembeds the raw state. Both show the eight most probable vocabulary tokens.
Inventory reconstructs the state using positive combinations of normalized J-lens directions. Coefficients are divided by the state norm; they are not probabilities. The leading retained atom labels each cell.
Follow a concept
Pin a word from any cell. The grid shows its rank, and the chart follows it through the four loops at your selected position. An exact trace matches all complete-token case variants. A missing saved top-8 match is labeled >8, never assigned an invented rank.
Compare carefully
The shaded workspace band is physical layers 24–40. Recorded research runs omit the four loop-end targets; empty cells mean missing data. Live runs include all 192 sites. Readouts describe this instrument’s reach, not a complete inventory of the model’s knowledge.
