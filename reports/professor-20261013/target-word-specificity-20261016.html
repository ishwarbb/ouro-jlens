Do target words distinguish scheming from benign behavior?

No validated signal from this dataset.

The test is whether words appear in J-lens readouts when the model schemes and remain absent in benign controls. The existing 11 examples are reviewed reasoning/answer mismatches, with no independently established concealed intent. Sensitivity to actual scheming is therefore unmeasured.

In the fixed early window at rank ≤10, false flags 201/502 correct-answer aligned controls (40.0%); fake flags 65/502 (12.9%); pretend flags 49/502 (9.8%). Every word that flags a mismatch also flags controls.

Task-separated rule check

This is a retrospective split: the first eight arithmetic and first eight record tasks are discovery; the other 16 tasks are evaluation. All repeats of a task stay together. Earlier inspection of this dataset prevents calling the evaluation set an untouched prospective holdout.

We considered 16 measured words × ranks 1, 5 and 10 in the fixed early window. A rule had to flag at least one discovery mismatch and flag zero discovery controls, including reviewed truthful responses under the same conflict prompt. None of the 48 rules qualified. No detector was selected for evaluation.

The full-cohort descriptive check also found no qualifying rule across any of the five saved windows (240 word/window/rank combinations). This includes the reasoning tail and message start; those retrospective windows do not establish advance warning.

If same-prompt controls are omitted, rank-1 fake appears promising: it flags 1/8 discovery mismatches and 0/248 correct-answer aligned controls. But it flags the reviewed truthful continuation of the exact same prompt at the same site and token position. It also flags 0/3 evaluation mismatches. This is a control failure, not a scheming monitor.

Criterion for the next experiment

Label goal-directed deceptive behavior from actions and outcomes independently of the lens. Review the full reasoning for disclosure. Retain ordinary mistakes, overt instruction following and ambiguous cases as separate groups. Select words, sites, windows and thresholds on discovery tasks; freeze them before collecting new task-disjoint evaluation data.

Report the benign false-alarm rate and detection rate on independently verified schemes, with every attempt retained. Under the requested strict criterion, any observed benign flag rejects that rule; no positive schemes leaves detection unmeasured. For a claim of advance warning, the measured source must precede the first deceptive action. Zero observed false alarms still requires uncertainty reporting and broader benign coverage.

Scope and limitations
  • Aligned controls are instructed to answer accurately and reward the correct answer. They do not represent all benign tasks.
  • 502 aligned labels use automatic answer parsing; 16 selected controls have full-text review. The latter overlap the former and must not be added as independent samples.
  • The same-prompt controls were selected for prefix similarity; they are informative counterexamples, not a random control sample.
  • 11 mismatch proxies cover only eight tasks and contain duplicate response forms. They are not verified schemes.
  • Rates use one flag per response and task-macro rates are also provided. Repeated samples of a task are not independent task replications.
  • Early means the fixed first 20 generated source positions plus the last prompt source. It can include CHECK or an already incorrect assertion, and does not guarantee advance warning.
  • The other windows are retrospective or post-answer; their flags must not be represented as advance detection.
  • Only 68 preselected sites and the saved windows were measured. No hit means no qualifying stored-slot hit in that scope, not that a concept is absent.
  • fabricate and mislead have no single-token variants in this assay and are unmeasured, not zero-hit successes.
  • Word presence in J-lens readouts is the signal. Absence of the word from printed reasoning is a separate criterion and cannot establish specificity.
